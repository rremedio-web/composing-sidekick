# Working theory — scoped to the owner's practice (template)

_Not a textbook. Theory that explains why the owner's moves work, so composed material
(gears 5-6) uses the same physics instead of imitating surfaces. Write it after
`idiom.md` is filled; every claim here should point at a measured or owner-confirmed
line there. Source-based study cards (reading notes on foreground/continuity, scales of
form, repetition, expectation, evolving sound) belong in `docs/research/` and are linked
from here, with what was actually read stated._

These are working interpretations and possible exercises, not universal rules the
owner has approved. Check the current piece, their direct notes and the
[shared knowledge guide](../../../../docs/KNOWLEDGE_GUIDE.md). Corpus measurements need
their build/version context; they do not impose register, timing or form.

## Modal gravity
- Why the owner's home progressions work without dominant pull: name the mechanism
  (plagal gravity, shared tones, a half-step shadow) for each oscillation in `idiom.md`.
- Which colour chords are inflections and which could establish a new centre; what
  (bass, phrasing, cadence) would be needed for the latter.
- Reharmonisation rule of thumb: preserve common tones, move the fewest voices.

## Melody physics
- The cells and answers that fit the idiom; one exercise per cell, stated as an option,
  not a rule every authored leap must satisfy.
- The default cadence (how a phrase closes without brightening) and when to break it.
- Repetition rhetoric: repeat a cell until it becomes floor, THEN change one note —
  the change reads huge because the floor was established.
- Register: the corpus median is a description. Choose register from the current
  instrument, foreground role and sounding parts; a higher return may be deliberate.

## Rhythm and feel physics
- Metric hierarchy in the owner's grooves: compose velocity CONTOUR, not velocity
  values; off-beats lighter, not silent.
- Push vs drag: effects depend on the sound and pattern. Preserve authored timing; never
  apply a corpus-wide offset to every part. If the owner's syncopation comes from a
  delay return, write parts straight and check routing before adding MIDI offsets.
- Where the business lives (above the kick, in hats/leads) and what stays law.

## Energy physics (song scale)
- Establish each piece's form from its material and the owner's intent. A climb, dip,
  peak and descent is one possible form, not a required one; the owner may reject the
  feeling of one continuous build.
- Energy can move through volume, sends, filter cutoff, role entry/exit, register,
  rhythm and harmony. Decide which dimension changes and which holds.
- Endings: name the owner's ending shapes (decay, subtraction, hard stop) with examples
  from their finished tracks.

## When composing (gear 5) — the contract
1. Fix key/register/groove from the seed or brief BEFORE writing notes.
2. Build from a cell (the owner's, or new in their vocabulary), establish by
   repetition, earn one surprise, resolve it stepwise or by silence.
3. State velocity and timing intent alongside pitches. Uniform attacks can be
   deliberate in a mechanical part; do not humanise by default.
4. Use the tool critics as diagnostics within their workflow. Passing a score is not
   musical proof; a rejection does not invalidate the owner's authored tensions.
   Reconsider the cell when repeated revisions fail the musical purpose.
5. Deliver with one-sentence explanations per choice, provenance, assumptions.
