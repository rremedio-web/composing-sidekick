# Ideas bank — reusable composing moves (ours, not borrowed) — template

_General moves the sidekick proposes while composing with the owner. Not tied to any
song: each entry says when it fits and how to do it. **Status** records the owner's ear
only (untested / kept / dropped, with date) — never a metric. Which song an idea was
tried in lives in that piece's notes (`docs/pieces/<PIECE>.md`), not here. Other
artists' moves go in `borrowed-techniques.md`; the owner's own habits in `idiom.md`.
Add an idea the first time it is proposed; update its status when the owner judges it.
The public repository ships this file without the author's entries._

Entry format:

```markdown
- **Name of the move.** What it is, in one or two sentences, in producer language.
  _Fits when:_ the situation that calls for it.
  _Status:_ untested (first proposed YYYY-MM-DD) | kept (YYYY-MM-DD, the owner's words) | dropped (YYYY-MM-DD, why)
```

## Melody and lines
_(e.g. answering a call in its gaps; a top line above the highest sounding note; three
variants that each change one thing; a peak line that hands over to the next section)_

## Drums and arrangement
_(e.g. one kit per section when layering obscures its role; copy the owner's clips to
build a spine and write new only for the gaps; kick alone first and last; a pickup fill
taken from the owner's own pattern; check which subdivision already exists before adding
hats; ask which drum language a piece wants before reusing a formula; the peak must earn
being the peak)_

## Method
- **Measure the bounce before arguing structure.** Per section: RMS, share of energy
  below 120 Hz, share above 4 kHz, transient rate. These describe measured energy and
  activity, not whether the low end is masked, percussion is perceived, or a climax
  succeeds. Reconcile with listening and the owner's judgment before proposing a repair.
  _Fits when:_ there is a bounce and the discussion is about energy, drums or mix.
- **Give a listener only neutral facts.** When asking a listener model to hear a
  bounce, pass tempo and bar length, never the section map or expectations — otherwise
  it confirms the map instead of hearing. _Fits when:_ every listen request.
