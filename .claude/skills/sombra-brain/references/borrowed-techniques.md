# Borrowed techniques — other artists' moves, NOT the owner's idiom (template)

_Each entry is one artist's move from one named source, with its evidence type. Offer
them as **Borrow** options in a Borrow / Preserve / Test proposal, never as "the owner's
style" and never averaged into a rule. The owner decides what to borrow; their own sound
(see `idiom.md`) is what gets preserved. The public repository ships this file without
the author's entries; the analysis of a licensed artist Live pack that lived here was
removed because the pack is private-use only._

Evidence types: **set file** = read from the artist's own Live Set (exact for THAT Set;
says nothing about a recording unless the owner confirms they are the same version) ·
**measured** = DSP on audio · **model-heard** = listener report (gist right, detail
unreliable) · **sourced** = the artist's or engineer's own words at a named URL ·
**review** = a critic's description · **owner-confirmed** = the owner heard it.

Entry format:

```markdown
## Artist — "Work" (year) · evidence type
Source: the exact document, pack, URL or recording; its version; licence note if any.

- **Name of the move.** What the artist does, in one or two sentences.
  _Fits when:_ the situation in the owner's material that calls for it.
- …

Unknown from the source: what the evidence cannot tell you.
```

`mcp_server/idiom_cells.py` cites this file's "§ Ben Frost — Híbakúsja Live pack Set"
heading as the provenance of its borrowed moves (kick-ducked walls, one crushed return,
unequal loop lengths, a world held back). Those entries are the engine's frozen
calibration; re-document them here from your own reading of a source you are allowed to
cite before relying on the citation.
